# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps:

- Being kind and respectful to other people
- Accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- Taking responsibility, apologising to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the people using this project

Behaviour that does not:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their
  explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## A note specific to this project

This project gives AI agents access to production websites. Discussions about safety, host
policies, and failure modes are the whole point, and disagreement about them is welcome and
useful.

Two things are not:

- **Encouraging anyone to bypass a host's stated policy.** If a host forbids something, the
  answer here is to route around it or confirm with the host — not to advise someone to do it
  anyway and hope.
- **Reporting a security issue in public.** Use the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers through a
[GitHub issue](https://github.com/billylui/wordpress-performance-skills/issues) for anything
public, or a
[security advisory](https://github.com/billylui/wordpress-performance-skills/security/advisories/new)
for anything sensitive. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards, and will communicate
the reasons for any moderation decision.
